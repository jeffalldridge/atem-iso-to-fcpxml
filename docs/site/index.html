From Recording to Final Cut.

  1. Install the App

    Download the disk image, drag ATEM ISO to FCPXML to Applications, and open it. That is the whole installation.

  2. Open Your Recording

    Mount the ATEM SSD and drop the recording folder on the window, or click Choose Recording…. Double-clicking the .drp inside the folder works too.

  3. Pick Cameras and Audio

    The cameras you switched to live are already checked. Choose the WAV for the soundtrack and add the mic inputs as extra angles if you want them.

  4. Open in Final Cut

    Click Create Final Cut XML, then Open in Final Cut Pro. You get an event with the multicam clip, the live edit, and every source file. Your original files stay untouched.

Compatibility

ATEM Mini Pro ISO · 1080p60 · Final Cut Pro 12.4

Verified with two real recordings imported into Final Cut. The app needs macOS 15 or later; the XML targets Final Cut Pro 10.6 and later. Other ATEM ISO models, frame rates, and firmware follow the same documented format but remain untested on real media.

Questions

Do I Need Python, Homebrew, or DaVinci Resolve?

No. ATEM ISO to FCPXML is a signed, notarized Mac app with nothing to install besides itself. It reads the .drp the ATEM wrote and the headers of the MP4 and WAV files directly. A one-file Python version exists for the command line if you prefer it.

Does It Use the WAV Files?

Yes. Each camera angle pairs the camera's ISO MP4 with its own WAV from Audio Source Files, so the compressed audio inside the MP4 is never used. You choose one soundtrack for the live edit, and the mic inputs can be added as separate audio angles.

Can I Rename My Recording or Inputs?

Yes. Custom input labels and a renamed recording folder work. Keep the files and subfolders inside the recording intact. Use the .drp written by the ATEM; a project exported from Resolve is a different format.

Does It Copy My Footage?

No. The app writes one .fcpxml beside your .drp and leaves the media where it is. Final Cut's import settings decide whether footage is copied or linked. Keep the SSD connected until copying finishes, or while editing linked footage.

Will It Import into My Selected Event?

Importing the XML creates a new event, named after the recording folder, with the multicam clip, the Live Edit project, and the source clips. You can move them to another event afterward.

What Should I Check Before Exporting?

Check audio sync, the end of the recording, and any markers in the timeline. The app warns about timing differences but does not correct them. Keyed sections use the ATEM program recording, or the first camera if that recording is missing. Stills swapped during recording may be missing.
