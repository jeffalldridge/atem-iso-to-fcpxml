Free, open-source Mac utility for Blackmagic ATEM Mini ISO

Convert ATEM Mini ISO recordings to a Final Cut Pro multicam.

Right-click the .drp the ATEM wrote next to its recording. Get a multicam clip with every camera's ISO picture and uncompressed WAV sound, plus a timeline of the switch you performed live, cuts and dissolves included. No DaVinci Resolve, no transcoding, no uploads.

How it works

1. Record ISO

Record to an SSD with ISO enabled on an ATEM Mini Pro ISO, Mini Extreme ISO, SDI ISO or Television Studio HD8 ISO. The switcher writes a .drp log of every cut, transition, key and fade next to the program recording.

2. Right-click, convert

Mount the SSD, right-click the recording folder, choose Quick Actions → ATEM ISO to FCPXML. Pick the inputs you used (the ones you switched to are pre-selected) and the WAV for the soundtrack.

3. Import and finish

Import the .fcpxml into Final Cut Pro. Trim, fix a bad switch in the Angle Viewer, swap the soundtrack to the sound board feed, add titles, and export your video and audio podcasts.

What the ATEM did, and what Final Cut gets

On the ATEMIn the Final Cut timeline
Cut between inputsCut between multicam clips, frame-accurate
Mix transition (auto or T-bar)Editable Cross Dissolve of the same length
Dip, wipe, DVE, stingerCross Dissolve plus a marker (or the rendered program picture, your choice)
Media Player stillIts own angle
Key on air, fade to black, colour barsThat stretch of the program recording as an angle, with a marker
AudioAlways the WAV of the soundtrack angle you chose; never the MP4's AAC

The ISO MP4s only carry the ATEM's program mix. The WAVs in Audio Source Files are each input's own 24-bit audio before the mixer, which is why the converter pairs every camera with its WAV and offers the mic inputs as angles. Alignment uses the timecode and Broadcast Wave time reference the ATEM writes into every file.

Install once. Convert on your Mac.

Quick Action

Download the zip, double-click ATEM ISO to FCPXML.workflow, click Install. It is a plain Automator workflow with the converter inside; open it in Automator to read every line.

Needs Python 3.9+. If macOS asks to install the Command Line Tools the first time, say yes.

Command line

git clone https://github.com/jeffalldridge/atem-iso-to-fcpxml.git
python3 atem-iso-to-fcpxml/atem_iso_to_fcpxml.py "/Volumes/ATEM SSD/Sunday Service"
Options: --cameras 1,2, --audio "MIC 1", --transitions cut, --dry-run and more in the README.

Questions before you convert

Is the ATEM .drp a DaVinci Resolve project?

Not really. It is a newline-delimited JSON log of the switcher's state that Resolve knows how to open. This converter reads it directly, so Resolve is never involved. The format is written up in the repository docs.

Does it touch my recording?

No. It reads the log and the file headers, and writes one new .fcpxml next to the .drp. Final Cut references the media where it is, so keep the SSD mounted or copy the folder to your edit drive first.

Will it work with my ATEM, frame rate or Final Cut version?

It was verified on ATEM Mini Pro ISO recordings at 1080p60 with Final Cut Pro 12.4, and writes FCPXML 1.10, which Final Cut 10.6 and later read. Other models and rates follow the same documented format and are covered by synthetic tests; please report what you find.

What about lower thirds and keys?

Keys are not rebuilt as Final Cut effects. Those stretches show the ATEM program recording so nothing is lost, and a marker tells you where. Redoing a title on the clean ISO picture usually looks better anyway.

Why not just use Resolve?

You can. This exists for people who prefer Final Cut Pro's speed, especially for exporting a weekly service as a video and an audio podcast.
